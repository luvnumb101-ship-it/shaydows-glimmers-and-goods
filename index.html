Our Story

◈ ◆ ◈

Shaydow’s Glimmers & Goods grew from grief, love, and the quiet rituals that make life gentler — baking, noticing light, creating beauty in small ways. Every item and image here is made slowly, lovingly, and in honor of Shaydow, my black American Shorthair who taught me how to notice joy.
